Our Academic Programs

A rigorous, comprehensive curriculum designed to prepare students for a changing world.

Primary School (Grades K-5)

Focusing on foundational literacy, numeracy, and social-emotional growth through structured play and inquiry-based learning.

  • Core Subjects: Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies
  • Specialties: Art, Music, Physical Education, Introductory Coding

Middle School (Grades 6-8)

Bridging the gap between foundational concepts and critical thinking, encouraging independent problem-solving and collaboration.

  • Core Subjects: Pre-Algebra/Algebra, Earth & Life Sciences, World History
  • Electives: Foreign Languages, Robotics, Creative Writing, Drama

High School (Grades 9-12)

A college-preparatory environment offering robust advanced placements, honors tracks, and comprehensive career counseling.

  • Advanced Placement (AP) Tracks & Honors Options
  • Dedicated College Readiness & SAT/ACT Preparation Workshops
